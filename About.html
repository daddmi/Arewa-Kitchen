About Arewa Kitchen

A suya kitchen that cares about the fire, the spice and the plate in your hands.

Our Story

Arewa Kitchen is a suya kitchen. We season every stick ourselves, grill it over open fire and serve it while it is still smoking.

Suya is simple food, which means there is nowhere to hide. The meat has to be good, the yaji has to be balanced and the fire has to be right. We watch all three.

The menu has grown to include chicken suya, a suya pizza, a suya burger, masa and a jollof combo. The spice behind all of them is the same.
